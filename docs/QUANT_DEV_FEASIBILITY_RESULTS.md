## What actually ran

- All 50 previously completed issuer triples in the prior 64-case development roster.
- Their three-source records represent 150 annual disclosures. Fourteen original
  triples had no completed derived row and were not reconstructed or read from raw
  caches; their metadata was included in the parent manifest guard and their absence
  remains explicit in the parent-roster reconciliation.
- Eight previously computed lexical-family mention counts, not extracted issuer KPI values.
- 800 compatible count comparisons: 50 sequences x 8 families x 2 updates. These
  include zero differences and are **not 800 economic discoveries or changed KPIs**.
- Fifty prefix-causal path transformations using declared stored timestamps,
  with a clock channel; the original count levels remain in the private projected input.
- An explicit all-nine input-support assessment, not nine economic model evaluations.

The cohort was exposed by the prior exploratory study and conditioned on its wall-stop
completion prefix. It is not a new random sample, held-out cohort or independent test.

## Decision from this execution

**Do not spend effort fitting seven unsupported empirical models to this artifact.**
Feature computation worked; it did not create economic observations, market expectations
or predictive targets. The current representation is sparse and redundant in this
particular exposed cohort, not evidence that the underlying research ideas are false.

The next substantive prerequisite is independently reviewable economic measurement
and qualified public-history coverage under the [gold-dependent pilot](QUANT_EMPIRICAL_PILOT.md).
That pilot remains blocked on unassigned independent reviewers and its other source,
curation, rights and resource prerequisites. We did not relabel this mechanical pass
as its completion. The count-only worker partition is not the full reviewable filing
partition required by that pilot. Return studies additionally retain their timestamp, identity,
complete-return and inference gates.
