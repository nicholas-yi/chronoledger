## 1. First answer a measurement question, not all nine return questions

Proposed distinct profile: **CL-QF-M0-PROPOSED**. It is not formal H1, H4 or H6,
does not replace their thresholds/estimands and cannot clear D6/D13.

Question: can independent reviewers define a compatible issuer-specific economic
state and determine whether an update is genuinely new within a specified observed
public history? What fraction remains unresolved because identity, units, definition,
source coverage or chronology is ambiguous?

This is a measurement-feasibility pilot. It does not estimate alpha, causal disclosure
effects, investor expectations, cash-flow outcomes, financial risk-model performance
or executable portfolio utility.

The current toolkit consumes declared observations; it is **not an NLP extractor**.
Human-oracle inputs can evaluate representation/annotation feasibility, but cannot
be presented as automatic extraction accuracy. An automatic extractor would need
a separately frozen implementation/model/version before a distinct held label set
could evaluate it.

## 2. Population, units and exact calendar boundary

Proposed sampling unit: issuer, with one short sequence of dated eligible disclosures
per issuer. Target 30-50 issuer sequences, subject to a new approved eligibility
inventory; no claim that enough sequences currently exist.

Every included source, context, acceptance/knowledge timestamp and period end must
be inside **2012-01-01 through 2018-12-31, inclusive**, using the existing shared
document scope check before any body, derived text or byte fingerprint is read.
Unknown/unparseable dates fail closed. No year-only eligibility or price-cutoff proxy.

All H4/H6 validation sources in 2019-2021, holdout sources in
2022-01-01 through 2025-06-30, their follow-up envelopes, and the D1 return holdout
beginning 2024-07-01 remain excluded. A 2018 observation does not authorize a 2019
follow-up document. No labels/outcomes from those windows may enter this pilot.

Use only a declared, frozen eligible universe. The earlier annual-triple development
inventory does not establish a supply of multi-form, first-publication-qualified
trajectories. A contemporary ticker list is not the historical sampling frame.

## 3. Source and access prerequisites

Before any real inventory/read:

1. User approves the exact source types/fields, calendar, resource budget and
   inventory/curation operation. This plan does not lift any current hold.
2. Resolve retention, permitted processing, independent-review and nonreconstructive
   output rights. Free access or a public URL is not sufficient.
3. Use a reviewed, content-pinned **physically development-only input partition**
   in an isolated reader environment, not the mixed companyfacts ZIP or whole bulk
   parquet with only a downstream filter.
4. Separately approve whoever/whatever creates that partition: parsing a mixed raw
   member during curation can itself expose reserved material. It is not a loophole
   by which the analytic worker or this plan may inspect the mixed archive.
5. Verify the partition manifest and complete per-document/context date receipts
   before body access. Date declarations do not establish true SEC UTC authority.

The proposed empirical measurement can label source chronology only to the level
actually qualified. Without historical clock/first-public-source qualification,
do not describe its output as investor-time surprise or historical tradability.

## 4. Sampling and freeze

After an approved metadata-only inventory of the approved partition:

- retain eligible issuers with enough sources for the specified sequence;
- stratify by source/section/metric availability without inspecting returns;
- select issuers by a seeded hash from the complete eligible roster;
- keep issuer sequences together; never split related documents across development
  and independent evaluation to inflate an effective sample size;
- freeze exact accession/source IDs, context, content hashes, versions, seed,
  exclusions and replacement policy in a commit before body reading;
- stop rather than silently replace unavailable or difficult cases.

Fewer than 30 eligible issuers would stop this proposed feasibility screen. That
is a proposed operational minimum, not a power calculation or a precision certificate.
No real inventory is authorized by writing it here.

## 5. Measurement contract and human truth

Restrict the first version to a small, prespecified issuer-owned metric family set
and compatible fact tuples: issuer, metric, period, unit, consolidation scope,
definition, source and declared knowledge time. The exact taxonomy must be approved
and frozen before source screening.

Two independent, outcome-blind reviewers and adjudication are needed for meaningful
gold. Reviewers receive only approved source/context evidence, not future prices,
fundamental labels, strategy outputs or the candidate's answer. Report disagreement
and unresolved cases rather than having the same AI system certify its own output.

Human review capacity, reviewer independence and budget are currently **unassigned**.
The user's earlier lack of annotation bandwidth is not repaired by assigning gold
status to model self-agreement or to these synthetic fixtures. If reviewers cannot
be secured under an approved plan, this gold-dependent pilot stays blocked.

Record:

- metric eligibility and issuer versus industry attribution;
- unit/scope/definition compatibility and actual reporting period;
- repeated, revised, withdrawn, absent and unreadable distinctions;
- exact source evidence and the earliest eligible observed public support;
- whether the public-history set is complete enough to judge novelty;
- source/identity/clock uncertainty and every abstention reason.

## 6. Comparisons and endpoints

First-stage endpoints are descriptive feasibility counts, not significance tests:

1. Eligible, available, independently labelled, resolved and unresolved issuer sequences.
2. Reviewer agreement and adjudication counts by metric/error class.
3. Coverage of compatible state updates and observable-public-history novelty.
4. Economic versus wording/unit/definition/coverage changes.
5. Provenance/period/identity errors and reconciliation to the full frozen roster.

Planned cheap rivals: dated numeric/structural diff; latest comparable same-period
fact; report-only novelty versus earlier-public-context novelty; endpoint/lag/time-gap
features versus order-sensitive coordinates. Human-provided inputs are labelled
oracle-input checks, not candidate-extractor successes.

No tuning on the independently labelled evaluation cases. Detector/model revisions
after an error analysis require a new version and separate future evaluation, keeping
the original results intact. Pilot size does not establish the full H1 annotation
requirements or valid precision at an arbitrarily high target.

## 7. Gates for the other eight directions

| Later empirical question | Additional prerequisites; none approved here |
|---|---|
| Informative disclosure timing | Complete observation opportunities, reporting calendar and censoring contract; timing qualification; calendar/renewal baselines |
| Signature increment | Enough independently labelled ordered events; causal embeddings; endpoint/lag/timing rivals; source-date precision that does not invent tie order |
| Network transmission | Dated two-ended identity, independently supported economic fractions and coverage; distinct exposure/similarity operators; common-shock controls |
| Cash-flow/discount-rate news | Economically justified state model, sufficiently long qualified history and outcome-source approval; no LLM-label substitution for identification |
| Complex-model increment | Frozen representation and matched cost/exposure rivals; dependency-aware out-of-sample design and inference |
| Robust decision value | Qualified mean/cost/uncertainty inputs, borrow/financing/fill constraints, complete economic returns and valid calibration |
| Partial identification | Justified support/selection assumptions and joint feasible worlds; allow unbounded/not-identified outcomes |
| Prospective evidence | New source/model/forecast freeze before outcomes, independent collection/settlement evidence and a justified statistical null |

The current monthly inference failures and limited independent time history are
not fixed by more labelled claims. Qualified price/terminal-return history, rights,
timestamp/identity qualification, inference calibration and economic margin remain
separate gates for any later return evaluation.

## 8. Operational plan and stop rules to approve later

Propose one frozen bounded pass, one compute CPU, at most 1 GiB, with a specific wall
budget and output caps set **before** approval. The actual empirical budget and human
allocation are not currently approved. No paid source, API key, model run or download
is requested by this document.

Stop on scope/date/rights/identity uncertainty, missing manifest evidence, unavailable
reviewer independence, resource cap, insufficient eligible population or corrupted
source. No auto-resume, replacements, source repair or fallback into the mixed archive.

Private outputs: casebook, scoped evidence/labels, reviewer/adjudication records and
source lineage under the approved access boundary. Public outputs: aggregate roster
reconciliation, error dictionary, method/version/source pins and limitations; no
raw issuer payloads or reconstructive licensed examples.

Whether a ledger row is required must be decided under the specific future study
contract. This measurement-only proposal appends none and grants no exception for
an eventual return evaluation.
